Led the frontend test migration from Karma/Jasmine to Vitest with Playwright coverage, cutting CI time and moving the codebase onto a modern TypeScript/ESM toolchain.

Stood up team-wide Datadog observability across the service mesh — RUM, APM, logs, custom SLOs — and tunes Kubernetes resource configs against live traffic, holding production incidents near zero. Runs a 30-day CVE remediation SLA and a rolling Angular major-version upgrade across every service, validated through formal change review. Built the Okta MFA integration for the agent-facing application, and is its SME and L2 escalation point.
